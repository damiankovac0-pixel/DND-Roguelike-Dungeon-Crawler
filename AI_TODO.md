## Boss Roster (Future Implementation)
Boss order: The Observer → Seraphine → Vorrak → Kaelros → Nyxara
(mysterious → creepy → brutal → legendary → reality-breaking)

### 1. The Observer — Shard of Sight (The Tower, floors 1-5)
- All-Seeing Gaze: Reveals invisible or hidden creatures.
- Petrifying Stare: Players who meet its gaze risk being slowed or turned partly to stone.
- Memory Beam: Deals psychic damage and makes the target forget a spell, ability, or recent action for 1 round.
- Tower's Judgment: Summons floating stone pillars that crush or block movement.

### 2. Seraphine, The Thorn Saint — Shard of Bloom (The Rotting Garden, floors 6-10)
- Thorn Halo: Damages anyone who attacks her in melee.
- Bloom of Decay: Flowers grow under players and explode into poison spores.
- Vine Grasp: Restrains players with living roots.
- False Blessing: Heals herself while poisoning enemies nearby.

### 3. Vorrak, The Ashen Maw — Shard of Hunger (The Cinder Wastes, floors 11-15)
- Devouring Bite: Heals when it damages a creature.
- Ash Breath: Creates a choking ash cloud that blinds and damages players.
- Molten Charge: Rushes forward, leaving burning ground behind.
- Hunger Frenzy: Gets stronger when below half health.

### 4. Kaelros, The Drowned King — Shard of Depths (The Sunken Halls, floors 16-20)
- Tide Command: Pushes or pulls players across the battlefield.
- Drowning Curse: Makes a target feel like they are suffocating even on land.
- Anchor Slam: Huge area attack that knocks creatures prone.
- Royal Guard: Summons drowned knights from pools of black water.

### 5. Nyxara, The Mirror Witch — Shard of Reflection (The Glass Labyrinth, floors 21-25)
- Mirror Double: Creates a copy of a player that uses weaker versions of their attacks.
- Shatter Step: Teleports between mirrors around the arena.
- Reflected Pain: Part of the damage dealt to her is reflected back.
- True Face: Forces a player to confront a fear or memory, causing psychic damage.
